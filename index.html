Proper Finger Placement

To type efficiently, place your fingers on the "home row" keys. The small bumps on the 'F' and 'J' keys will help you find your position without looking.

Left Hand

  • Pinky: A
  • Ring: S
  • Middle: D
  • Index: F

Right Hand

  • Index: J
  • Middle: K
  • Ring: L
  • Pinky: ;

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I increase my typing speed?

The key is consistent practice. Focus on accuracy first, and speed will naturally follow. Use the home row keys (ASDF JKL;) as your base, and make sure to use all your fingers.

How fast should I type?

An average typing speed is around 40 WPM. A good speed for most jobs is 50-60 WPM. Professional typists often exceed 70 WPM. Focus on steady improvement.

How is typing speed measured?

It's measured in WPM (Words Per Minute). In standard tests, one "word" is considered to be five characters, including spaces. The formula is: (Total Characters / 5) / Time in Minutes.

Why is it important to take a typing speed test?

It helps you benchmark your current skill level and track your improvement over time. Regularly testing yourself can motivate you to practice and identify weaknesses.
